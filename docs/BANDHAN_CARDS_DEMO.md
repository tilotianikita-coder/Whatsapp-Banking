# Bandhu — Bandhan Bank credit card onboarding (branch `bandhan-cards`, org `bandhan`)

DIY credit card onboarding on **WhatsApp and web**, modelled on the Axis "AXIA" demo (leadership deck slides 11–13):
an AI card advisor that recommends the right card from the customer's needs and eligibility, then takes them through
application, KYC and the decision, with lead management for drop-offs, an underwriter console and human handoff.

Code lives in `bandhan/` (its own package directory) and `scripts/bandhan/`; it does not depend on LoanSeva.
Org: alias `bandhan` (00Dbm000019HbVJEA0, https://trailsignup-fbf7dadaec8000.my.salesforce.com). Always pass `-o bandhan`.

## The cards and the eligibility logic
Benefits are Bandhan Bank's published ones (creditcards.bandhan.bank.in). **Fees and eligibility thresholds are demo
placeholders** in `Bandhu_Card__mdt` (generated by `scripts/bandhan/content.py`): replace them with the bank's
schedule of charges and credit policy.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sparks | Entry: everyday rewards | ₹499, waived on ₹1L/yr | ₹25,000 | 700 / 680 | 2× income (₹25K–₹1L) |
| Ignite | Groceries & travel | ₹999, waived on ₹2L/yr | ₹40,000 | 720 / 700 | 2.5× (₹50K–₹2.5L) |

| Lumina | Premium travel | ₹9,999, waived on ₹10L/yr | ₹2,00,000, or existing customer with ₹10L+ balances & FDs | 760 / 740 | 4× (₹3L–₹15L) |
| Shield | Secured against an FD | free | none | none | 90% of the FD |

- Age 21–60 salaried, 21–65 self-employed; CIBIL below 650 = no card.
- **Existing customers (ETB)**: income from salary credits, PAN/DOB/address on file, KYC on file (no Aadhaar / Video KYC),
  the bank's pre-approved card and limit, and the relationship route to Lumina.
- **New to bank (NTB)**: declared income verified through Account Aggregator (mock), Aadhaar eKYC and Video KYC (mocks).
- **Recommendation**: among eligible cards, the best fit to what they spend on (travel, international, dining, groceries,
  shopping, fuel), their fee preference and monthly spend; plus one alternative and a "you'd qualify for X with income Y" hint.
- **Decision** (assist-only, explainable): PAN, Aadhaar/KYC, CIBIL, income, Video KYC, fraud & dedupe, card policy.
  All pass → straight-through approval and a virtual card; any refer/pending → underwriter; any fail → decline with the
  best lower card offered.

## Existing customers and the bureau report (BandhuCustomer, BandhuBureau)
- **Tiers** from the bank's records: Wealth (balances + FDs + demat of ₹10L+), Loan (retail loan), Deposit (savings,
  salary, current, FD), Microfinance (microfinance loans only). Cards are offered per tier (`ETB_Tiers__c`): microfinance
  customers get Sparks only, and only on a clean repayment track. Relationship score /100: balance 30, vintage 25,
  products 25, repayment 20.
- **Holds before anything else**: a dormant / inoperative account, KYC not complete or an AML flag stops the journey
  (step `hold`), tells the customer what to do (AML is never disclosed: "a routine review") and gives the RM a task.
- **Silent soft pull** for existing customers on the PAN on file (the consent at the start covers it), cached on the
  Contact for 30 days; new-to-bank customers are still asked.
- **Full bureau report** (JSON on the journey and the check): tradelines, DPD, enquiries, utilisation, write-offs.
  Rules: below 650 or DPD over 90 in 24 months -> decline; write-off, settlement, 6+ enquiries in 6 months or 90%+
  utilisation -> manual review; no footprint -> refer.
- Underwriting has a new **Customer Due Diligence** check (8 checks now).

## Catalogue, Bandhan Score, limits and the secured card (phase 2)
- **Catalogue**: Sparks (Classic), Ignite (Silver), Flare (Gold), Lumina (Platinum), **Voyage** (co-branded travel;
  partner and benefits are PLACEHOLDERS) and **Shield** (secured against an FD, lifetime free, limit 90% of the FD).
- **Bandhan Score** (`BandhuScore`, model BSC-1.0): a 0-1000 scorecard of 14 factors (bureau 530, Account Aggregator
  260, income 90, relationship 80, channel 40). Missing data scores about half. Bands A 700+, B 550-699, C 400-549,
  D under 400 (manual review). Every decision logs each factor's value and points (`Score_Factors__c`, the new
  **Bandhan Score** check), so it can be explained to the customer or a regulator.
- **Limit**: income x the card's multiple (Sparks 2, Ignite 2.5, Flare / Voyage 3, Lumina 4), +0.5x band A, -0.5x band
  C, +0.25 / 0.5x for an existing customer's relationship score 50 / 70+, capped (entry 3x, premium 5x), then cut for
  FOIR; shown as a range (80% - 100%).
- **"You applied for X"**: typing a card you don't qualify for at the recommendation (e.g. *I want Lumina*) shows what
  was missed and the closest card at or below that tier, with what it gives.
- **Secured fallback**: no unsecured card (score, income, or new to credit with no AA data) -> Shield, with the FD sized
  for twice the monthly spend. At underwriting a weak bureau no longer declines a secured card; DPD 90+ refers.

## Account Aggregator cash-flow analysis (BandhuAccountAggregator, BandhuCashFlow)
New-to-bank customers who choose *Connect my accounts* give a consent (purpose credit underwriting, 24 months of
statements, valid 30 days). The mock FIU returns 24 months from two banks (HDFC-style and SBI-style JSON with different
keys, flags, date and amount formats); `BandhuCashFlow` normalises them, categorises every transaction from its narration
and derives:
- net monthly income (median of the last 6 months), income stability (0-100), trend year on year, EMIs and FOIR,
  outflow, surplus, average balance, monthly spend by card category, lifestyle signals, festive seasonality (self-employed);
- flags: unexplained large credits, round-trip transfers with one party, months without salary, declared vs seen income.

What it changes: the customer sees *What your statements show*; the card fit weights what they actually spend on
(card weight x 3 / 2 / 1 for 30% / 15% / 7% of card-able spend) and the "why" lines quote it; the limit drops to 75% for
FOIR over 40% and 50% over 50%; the underwriter's Income Verification check carries the full analysis (JSON in Report
Data) and any flag refers the application.

| PAN lever | Statements show |
|---|---|
| 5th letter T (e.g. ABCPT1234K) | frequent traveller: says *groceries*, gets **Flare** instead of Ignite |
| 5th letter D / G / F | dines out / family groceries & fuel / long commute |
| any other 5th letter | spends mirror what the customer said |
| digits ending 8 (ABCPS1238K) | EMIs at 55% of income: limit halved |
| digits ending 9 (ABCPS1239K) | round-trip transfers, a 4x-income credit, two months without salary: Refer |
| Self-employed | business inflows with a Durga Puja peak |

## Lead qualification and scoring (BandhuQualification)
Every refresh of a prospect's Lead runs seven stage gates (records `Bandhu_Lead_Check__c`, shown on the Lead page),
cheapest first; a knock-out stops the rest and makes the Lead **Unqualified / Cold** with no RM task or nudge.

| Gate | Source | Knock-out |
|---|---|---|
| Serviceability | PIN → `Bandhu_Region__mdt` (Kolkata, Rest of WB, North East, Bihar & Jharkhand, Odisha, Metros, Rest of India) | Andaman, Ladakh, Lakshadweep |
| Consent | DPDP + soft-check consent | — |
| Dedupe & Fraud | fraud & dedupe (mock) | negative list (PAN …X) |
| Credit Bureau | CIBIL soft pull (mock) | below 650 |
| Income | Account Aggregator / declared | below ₹25,000 |
| Employment | declared | — |
| Card Eligibility | BandhuEligibility | no card (e.g. age) |

- **Credit score** (0–100): CIBIL 40, income 20 (verified 20 / declared 10), fraud 15, employment 10, best eligible card 15,
  scaled over the gates with data (confidence High / Medium / Low by how many had data).
- **Intent score** (0–100): income 25, employment 10, monthly card spend 10, age 5, journey depth 40, needs 10.
- **Rating** from the average: 70+ Hot, 40–69 Warm, below 40 Cold. Lead fields: Card Stage Gate (Qualified / Waiting at X /
  Knocked out at X), Card Region, Serviceable, Credit Confidence. Owner: the region's RM (`RM_Username__c`), else Config RM.

## Demo flow (~25 min)
1. **Web DIY (5 min)** — open the web page on a phone or browser: hero, card range, **Talk to Bandhu** → language
   (English / हिंदी / বাংলা) → Find my card → consent → name, mobile, OTP → what you spend on (chips), monthly spend,
   fee preference → employment, income, company, PAN, DOB, PIN → soft CIBIL consent → Account Aggregator → **the
   recommendation with "why this card", an alternative, the upgrade hint and the card tiles** → apply → email, Aadhaar
   eKYC, Video KYC → review → **approved, virtual card issued**.
2. **WhatsApp (5 min)** — from 9063480755: *Hi* → the same journey on WhatsApp; type *BN* to switch to Bengali; stop after
   the recommendation (drop-off).
3. **Lead management (5 min)** — Bandhu Cards → Leads: Priya (Hot, Lumina), Arjun (Hot, Ignite), Moumita (Unqualified:
   knocked out at the Credit Bureau gate) and the presenter's own drop-off (after ~2 minutes). Open one: stage gate and journey path, the seven qualification gates,
   intent and credit scores with the breakdown, CIBIL, eligible and recommended cards, drop point, RM task → **Nudge on WhatsApp** → the phone gets the RM message →
   *Continue* resumes where it stopped.
4. **Underwriting (5 min)** — Card Applications → Sourav Ghosh (Flare, Refer: CIBIL 730 in the refer band): stage gates,
   each check's detail, recommendation and reasons, why this card → **Approve & issue card** (notes needed only against
   the recommendation) → the customer gets the approval with the masked virtual card on WhatsApp.
5. **Human handoff (4 min)** — Omni-Channel *Messaging - Available*; on WhatsApp type *Agent* → Bandhu hands over and
   logs a task → accept the chat: the Bandhu handoff panel shows who they are, new or existing, every answer, CIBIL,
   eligible cards, the recommendation and why they asked → reply from the console.
6. **Optional: existing customer** — seed `ON_DEMO_PHONE = 'Rohan'`: welcome back, no income proof or KYC, pre-approved
   Flare with a ₹3.5L limit; or `'Sutapa'`: Lumina through the relationship route.
