# Bandhu — Bandhan Bank credit card onboarding (branch `bandhan-cards`, org `bandhan`)

Step-by-step demo of every journey: [BANDHAN_DEMO_FLOW.md](BANDHAN_DEMO_FLOW.md).

DIY credit card onboarding on **WhatsApp and web**, modelled on the Axis "AXIA" demo (leadership deck slides 11–13):
an AI card advisor that recommends the right card from the customer's needs and eligibility, then takes them through
application, KYC and the decision, with lead management for drop-offs, an underwriter console and human handoff.

Code lives in `bandhan/` (its own package directory) and `scripts/bandhan/`; it does not depend on LoanSeva.
Org: alias `bandhan` (00Dbm000019HbVJEA0, https://trailsignup-fbf7dadaec8000.my.salesforce.com). Always pass `-o bandhan`.

## The cards and the eligibility logic
Benefits are Bandhan Bank's published ones (creditcards.bandhan.bank.in). **Fees and eligibility thresholds are demo
placeholders** in `Bandhu_Card__mdt` (generated by `scripts/bandhan/content.py`): replace them with the bank's
schedule of charges and credit policy.

| Card | Tier | Joining / annual fee | Min monthly income | CIBIL: approves / on review | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sparks | Entry: everyday rewards | ₹499, waived on ₹1L/yr | ₹25,000 | Good 700+ / Fair 650–699 | 2× income (₹25K–₹1L) |
| Ignite | Groceries & travel | ₹999, waived on ₹2L/yr | ₹40,000 | Good 700+ / Fair 650–699 | 2.5× (₹50K–₹2.5L) |
| Flare | Travel & lifestyle | ₹2,999, waived on ₹4L/yr | ₹75,000 | Good 700+ / Fair 650–699 | 3× (₹1L–₹5L) |
| Lumina | Premium travel | ₹9,999, waived on ₹10L/yr | ₹2,00,000, or existing customer with ₹10L+ balances & FDs | Excellent 750+ / Good 700–749 | 4× (₹3L–₹15L) |

- Age 21–60 salaried, 21–65 self-employed; CIBIL below 650 = no card.
- **Existing customers (ETB)**: income from salary credits, PAN/DOB/address on file, KYC on file (no Aadhaar / Video KYC),
  the bank's pre-approved card and limit, and the relationship route to Lumina.
- **New to bank (NTB)**: declared income verified through Account Aggregator (mock), Aadhaar eKYC and Video KYC (mocks).
- **Recommendation**: among eligible cards, the best fit to what they spend on (travel, international, dining, groceries,
  shopping, fuel), their fee preference and monthly spend; plus one alternative and a "you'd qualify for X with income Y" hint.
- **Decision** (assist-only, explainable): PAN, Aadhaar/KYC, CIBIL, income, Video KYC, fraud & dedupe, card policy.
  All pass → straight-through approval and a virtual card; any refer/pending → underwriter; any fail → decline with the
  best lower card offered.

## Agentforce: where AI is used (BandhuAI, Prompt Builder)
AI writes; the rules decide. Three Prompt Builder templates (Setup > Prompt Builder), run through the Einstein Trust Layer;
Apex assembles the facts, the templates hold the instructions (edit them there: publish a new version and activate it).
| Template | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Bandhu Card Answer** | web chat and WhatsApp, any step except personal details | free-form questions the keyword FAQ doesn't cover ("Can I use Flare in Dubai?"), answered only from the card catalogue, in the customer's language; then the pending question again. Never answers on their own approval, limit or score |
| **Bandhu Personal Note** | after the recommendation (*why*) and the issued card (*tip*) | a short note in the customer's language from their statements and the card's benefits. Arrives a few seconds after the turn (WhatsApp: a queued message; web: a second bubble) because the LLM can't run in the same transaction as the journey's saves |
| **Bandhu Underwriter Brief** | Card Application page, *✨ Generate brief* | summary, strengths, concerns (every Refer / Fail check and flag) and a suggested decision; saved on the application with its time. Advisory: the underwriter decides |
- WhatsApp runs on the **Bandhu Agentforce agent** (it routes every message to the journey actions); the web page calls the
  same journey engine directly, so both channels ask the same questions in the same order (checked turn by turn).
- If Einstein is unavailable the journey carries on with the rule-based text.

## Existing customers and the bureau report (BandhuCustomer, BandhuBureau)
- **Tiers** from the bank's records: Wealth (balances + FDs + demat of ₹10L+), Loan (retail loan), Deposit (savings,
  salary, current, FD), Microfinance (microfinance loans only). Cards are offered per tier (`ETB_Tiers__c`): microfinance
  customers get Sparks only, and only on a clean repayment track. Relationship score /100: balance 30, vintage 25,
  products 25, repayment 20.
- **Holds before anything else**: a dormant / inoperative account, KYC not complete or an AML flag stops the journey
  (step `hold`), tells the customer what to do (AML is never disclosed: "a routine review") and gives the RM a task.
- **Silent soft pull** for existing customers on the PAN on file (the consent at the start covers it), cached on the
  Contact for 30 days; new-to-bank customers are still asked.
- **Full bureau report** (JSON on the journey and the check): tradelines, DPD, enquiries, utilisation, write-offs.
  Rules: below 650 or DPD over 90 in 24 months -> decline; write-off, settlement, 6+ enquiries in 6 months or 90%+
  utilisation -> manual review; no footprint -> refer.
- Underwriting has a new **Customer Due Diligence** check (8 checks now).

| PAN digits ending | Bureau shows |
|---|---|
| 5 (ABCPS1235K) | no bureau footprint |
| 6 | 8 enquiries in 6 months (credit hungry) |
| 7 | settled credit card: manual review |
| 3 | card utilisation 92% |

Seed customers: `ON_DEMO_PHONE = 'Rina'` (microfinance only: Sparks) or `'Debasish'` (dormant: journey on hold).

## After the decision, and the branch (phase 4b)
- **Re-approach** (`BandhuPostIssue.reassess`, daily with the drop-off job): declined / not-eligible
  journeys are re-checked 6 months later (then every 3): a fresh soft pull (mock: +6 points per clean month, up to 60)
  and the same eligibility; if an unsecured card is now possible, an RM task and a WhatsApp *"you now qualify for X"*.
- **Limit enhancement**: cardholders 6+ months on book, activated, no missed payments, 40%+ utilisation get a higher
  limit (x1.25, x1.5 at 70%+, within the card's maximum) or, at the maximum, the next card up. On WhatsApp the customer
  replies *Yes*; nothing changes without that consent (logged). Behaviour fields (utilisation, payments, spend) stand in
  for the card management system feed.
- **Model monitoring**: outcomes by Bandhan Score band (issued, activated, missed payments, utilisation) saved as a
  quarterly CSV file (*Bandhu scorecard monitoring YYYY-Qn*) for recalibrating the scorecard. (No model is retrained
  automatically: the scorecard is transparent weights, adjusted by the risk team from this extract.)
- **RM 360** (Contact page in the Bandhu Cards app): tier, relationship score, products, holds, bureau (cached or a live
  soft view), cash flow, Bandhan Score and its weakest factors, and **the card to pitch** with the limit and why.
  *Start branch-assisted application* sends the customer the pitch on WhatsApp (branch channel scores higher); they give
  consent and the OTP there and continue.

Demo: to show a limit offer, on an issued application set Activated At and Card Issued At 6+ months back, Missed
Payments 0 and Avg Utilisation 75, then run `BandhuPostIssue.limitOffers()`; for a re-approach, set a declined
journey's Reassess On to today and Bureau Pulled At 7 months back, then run `BandhuPostIssue.reassess()`.

## Risk signals, income cross-checks and documents (phase 4a)
- **Dedupe & Fraud** (`BandhuRisk`): negative list, open applications on the PAN, application velocity (3+ journeys from
  the PAN or mobile in 24 hours, a device / session proxy), credit-hungry enquiries across 4+ lenders, and the declared
  city vs the PIN's city (mock geocoder). AA synthetic-income patterns are flagged by `BandhuCashFlow`.
- **Income cross-checks** on the Income Verification check: Form 26AS / AIS for everyone, GST (GSTIN, 12 months of
  returns, turnover vs declared income) for self-employed and business owners. Lever: PAN digits ending **2** -> GST filed
  7/12 months and AIS 45% below declared.
- **Documents** (Card Application page, *Income documents*): upload a salary slip, Form 16, ITR or bank statement -> read
  (OCR / NLP is a MOCK: figures simulated from the application; Einstein Document AI or an OCR vendor slots into
  `BandhuDocuments.extract`) -> PAN match and income cross-check; more than 15% below declared refers. A file name with
  *mismatch* reads 30% lower. Income Verification updates from the documents (and sets the verified income).

## Journey order and compliance (phase 3)
- **Order**: consent → name → OTP → **income source** (salaried / self-employed professional / business owner) →
  income → PAN → **Account Aggregator** ("connect your accounts for instant approval, no documents needed") → a live
  *"you qualify for N cards so far"* (soft, no bureau pull) → spend, monthly spend, fee → company, DOB, PIN → soft check
  → recommendation. Existing customers skip what is on file.
- **Consent log** `Bandhu_Consent__c` (related to the journey, application and customer): DPDP consent, soft check,
  AA (purpose, 24 months, expires in 30 days), the **hard enquiry** declared on the review screen before submission,
  **MITC delivered**, **card activation** (OTP), cancellation and withdrawal, each with the exact text shown and a DPDP
  retention date (PLACEHOLDER: 180 days for prospects, 5 years for cardholders).
- **RBI MD on Credit Cards 2022**: the approval includes the MITC link; *Activate* → OTP consent; unactivated cards close
  after 30 days (daily, with the drop-off job); *Cancel card* within the 7-day cooling-off period (PLACEHOLDER) closes
  it without charges; limit increases only with consent (phase 4).
- **Withdraw consent** anytime ("withdraw my consent" / "delete my data"): processing stops and the RM gets an erasure task.

## Catalogue, Bandhan Score and limits (phase 2)
- **Catalogue**: Bandhan's four cards only (creditcards.bandhan.bank.in): Sparks (Classic), Ignite (Silver), Flare (Gold),
  Lumina (Platinum). Bandhan has no secured or co-branded card: travel-heavy customers are matched to Flare / Lumina;
  when no card is possible, Bandhu says so, offers a specialist and mentions the **Loan / Overdraft against Term
  Deposit**, and the journey is re-checked in 6 months. (The secured-card logic stays dormant: it switches on only if a
  card with Secured = true is added to `Bandhu_Card__mdt`.)
- **Bandhan Score** (`BandhuScore`, model BSC-1.0): a 0-1000 scorecard of 14 factors in three groups: credit bureau
  600, bank statements (Account Aggregator) 250, income / relationship / channel 150. Missing data scores about half. Bands A 700+, B 550-699, C 400-549,
  D under 400 (manual review). Every decision logs each factor's value and points (`Score_Factors__c`, the
  **Bandhan Score** check), so it can be explained to the customer or a regulator.
- **Limit**: income x the card's multiple (Sparks 2, Ignite 2.5, Flare 3, Lumina 4), +0.5x band A, -0.5x band C,
  +0.25 / 0.5x for an existing customer's relationship score 50 / 70+, capped (entry 3x, premium 5x), then cut for FOIR;
  shown as a range (80% - 100%).
- **"You applied for X"**: typing a card you don't qualify for at the recommendation (e.g. *I want Lumina*) shows what
  was missed and the closest card at or below that tier, with what it gives.

## Account Aggregator cash-flow analysis (BandhuAccountAggregator, BandhuCashFlow)
New-to-bank customers who choose *Connect my accounts* give a consent (purpose credit underwriting, 24 months of
statements, valid 30 days). The mock FIU returns 24 months from two banks (HDFC-style and SBI-style JSON with different
keys, flags, date and amount formats); `BandhuCashFlow` normalises them, categorises every transaction from its narration
and derives:
- net monthly income (median of the last 6 months), income stability (0-100), trend year on year, EMIs and FOIR,
  outflow, surplus, average balance, monthly spend by card category, lifestyle signals, festive seasonality (self-employed);
- flags: unexplained large credits, round-trip transfers with one party, months without salary, declared vs seen income.

What it changes: the customer sees *What your statements show*; the card fit weights what they actually spend on
(card weight x 3 / 2 / 1 for 30% / 15% / 7% of card-able spend) and the "why" lines quote it; the limit drops to 75% for
FOIR over 40% and 50% over 50%; the underwriter's Income Verification check carries the full analysis (JSON in Report
Data) and any flag refers the application.

| PAN lever | Statements show |
|---|---|
| 5th letter T (e.g. ABCPT1234K) | frequent traveller: domestic travel plus 2 trips abroad a year; says *groceries*, gets **Flare** instead of Ignite |
| 5th letter D / G / F | dines out / family groceries & fuel / long commute |
| any other 5th letter | a balanced household (groceries, shopping, fuel, dining, a little travel; nothing abroad) |
| digits ending 8 (ABCPS1238K) | EMIs at 55% of income: limit halved |
| digits ending 9 (ABCPS1239K) | round-trip transfers, a 4x-income credit, two months without salary: Refer |
| Self-employed | business inflows with a Durga Puja peak |

## Lead qualification and scoring (BandhuQualification)
Every refresh of a prospect's Lead runs seven stage gates (records `Bandhu_Lead_Check__c`, shown on the Lead page),
cheapest first; a knock-out stops the rest and makes the Lead **Unqualified / Cold** with no RM task or nudge.

| Gate | Source | Knock-out |
|---|---|---|
| Serviceability | PIN → `Bandhu_Region__mdt` (Kolkata, Rest of WB, North East, Bihar & Jharkhand, Odisha, Metros, Rest of India) | Andaman, Ladakh, Lakshadweep |
| Consent | DPDP + soft-check consent | — |
| Dedupe & Fraud | fraud & dedupe (mock) | negative list (PAN …X) |
| Credit Bureau | CIBIL soft pull (mock) | below 650 |
| Income | Account Aggregator / declared | below ₹25,000 |
| Employment | declared | — |
| Card Eligibility | BandhuEligibility | no card (e.g. age) |

- **Credit score** (0–100): CIBIL 40, income 20 (verified 20 / declared 10), fraud 15, employment 10, best eligible card 15,
  scaled over the gates with data (confidence High / Medium / Low by how many had data).
- **Intent score** (0–100): income 25, employment 10, monthly card spend 10, age 5, journey depth 40, needs 10.
- **Rating** from the average: 70+ Hot, 40–69 Warm, below 40 Cold. Lead fields: Card Stage Gate (Qualified / Waiting at X /
  Knocked out at X), Card Region, Serviceable, Credit Confidence. Owner: the region's RM (`RM_Username__c`), else Config RM.
